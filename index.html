Game rules

Six reels, five rows, 19 fixed lines. Three or more matching symbols starting from the leftmost reel pay. Only the highest-paying combination on each line is awarded, at its longest consecutive match. All 19 lines are included in the displayed stake. Payouts below multiply the total stake per line; winning lines are added.

Wilds and Royal Reels

The Crown Ball Wild and Royal Reel Wild substitute for regular symbols, never for a Hoop Scatter. Six Wild/Royal symbols on a line pay 50× before multipliers. Shorter all-Wild runs do not pay on their own. A Royal Reel covers all five cells. Its multiplier applies only to lines whose winning matching prefix crosses that reel. If a winning prefix crosses multiple Royal Reels, their multipliers are added. A line with no Royal Reel uses 1×. Base rounds have at most two Royal Reels.

Free Spins and Momentum

Three Hoop Scatters trigger 8 Free Spins; four trigger 10 with one initial sticky Royal Reel; five or more trigger 12 with two. Royal Reels stay for the feature. Their values are drawn from the original round library. Royal multipliers available in this model are 2×, 3×, 5×, 8×, 12×, 20×, 40× and 250×; different feature states use subsets.

Each collected Hoop Scatter in Free Spins fills one meter point. Every four points awards up to two additional spins and adds a Royal Reel if a reel is available. Meter awards affect the next spin. At most 24 Free Spins can be awarded per feature; Royal Reels are limited to six. New Royal Reels may also arrive during a feature.

Momentum starts at 1×, increases by 1 after each winning free spin to a maximum of 5×, and resets to 1× after a miss. The current Momentum multiplies the sum of line payouts after Royal multipliers. The displayed Momentum is for the next free spin. Free Spins remain part of the original paid round and require no new stake.

RTP, distribution and maximum

The single paid base mode costs 1× the selected stake. Its theoretical RTP is exactly 92.00%, including all free-spin outcomes, for the supplied court-92-v1 weighted complete-round library. This is not the RTP of independent uniformly drawn reel symbols. Changing weights, outcomes or payouts invalidates this figure. RTP describes a long-run mathematical expectation, not an individual session return.

The maximum total payout per paid round is 50,000× the stake, including its feature. When the cap is reached, only the remaining amount is awarded and the round ends immediately. Displayed uncapped line combinations may sum higher; the cap takes precedence.

Controls

SPIN or Space starts a round. During a feature SPIN continues an existing reveal; free reveals normally advance automatically. +/− changes the stake between rounds. AUTO asks before up to 10 paid rounds; STOP AUTO prevents new paid rounds. Ongoing free reveals belong to the existing round. TURBO shortens animations. Sound starts off; ♪ toggles all generated audio. INFO opens these rules. Opening Info or hiding the page stops autoplay. Stake is locked during a round. Jurisdiction flags may disable some controls. Unsupported languages fall back to English.
